I made this for a workshop I'm teaching at UofT AI. Every dictation app I looked at was paid, which is a strange thing to ask of students who just want their lectures written down. So I took a lightweight speech-to-text model that runs on the Mac's Neural Engine and wrapped it in an app. It lives in the notch, transcribes lectures and meetings live, and keeps a library of everything it heard. Nothing leaves the machine, and it costs nothing.
